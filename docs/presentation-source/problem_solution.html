Problem & Solution

The Problem

Fragmented Hiring

Driver recruitment is often scattered across informal channels, leading to unverified credentials and slow placement.

Security Gaps

Sensitive documents and contact details are often exposed publicly or handled through insecure messaging.

The Solution

Unified Workflow

A single secure platform connecting candidates, transport employers, and administrators through one API.

Role-Based Privacy

Strict backend authorization ensures contact details and documents are only visible to verified, shortlisted employers.
